Principles

What holds the work together.

Evidence over adjectives

If a claim needs an adjective, it needs evidence instead. Every recommendation traces back to a requirement, a risk, or a record.

Phase-appropriate, always

Fit for where you are, from paper-based systems to predictive analytics. Nothing heavier than your stage needs, nothing lighter than an inspector expects.

Principals, not a bench

Both founders stay on the engagement from the first call to the final evidence. No handoff to juniors once the work begins.
