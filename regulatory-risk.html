What’s included

What the assessment covers.

The readiness assessment is a fixed-scope, fixed-fee engagement. We map every innovation use case across your quality system, classify each one by GxP impact and rank the exposure it creates. You come away knowing where the gaps are, what to fix first, and what it will take to stand in front of an inspector with confidence.

01

Use-case inventory

Every innovation use case in play or planned, from automation to AI, in one list.

02

GxP classification

Each use case classified by GxP impact, so the right expectations apply to it.

03

Risk ranking

Ranked by system and data type, so the riskiest items get attention first.

04

Data integrity review

How data is created, changed and kept, checked against ALCOA+ expectations.

05

Gap snapshot

Where current practice falls short of what you would need to show an inspector.

06

Recommended roadmap

A phase-appropriate sequence of next steps, with cost you can plan around.
